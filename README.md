# Bars North

Scottish hip hop: find it, hear it, share it. A prototype that runs in your browser and works offline once opened.

**Live:** https://comiccoder23.github.io/bars-north/

- Real names are public listings from open sources (each with its source link). Those people have not joined or checked their entries.
- Tracks by "DJ-Al-CC23" are made with AI and are free starter beats.
- Everything you save stays on your own device.

**Want to be removed or corrected?** Open an issue here ("Removal or correction request") and say which name. That is all that is needed.
